Dr. Bottenhorn will be sharing findings from her NIH BRAIN Initiative K99/R00, from a deep learning model of hormone-related gray matter morphology to patterns of structure-function coupling that connect early hormone differences to individual differences in functional connectivity gradients.

The BottenLab is once again recruiting grad students through [FIU's Cognitive Neuroscience PhD program](https://case.fiu.edu/psychology/phd-in-cognitive-neuroscience/index.html). Applications are due December 1st, 2026 for a Fall 2027 start. We will have a couple more positions coming on line soon, for a 2-year postdoc and a part-time lab manager.

If you're interested, please email your CV and a short blurb about why you'd like to join us to bottenlab@gmail.com.

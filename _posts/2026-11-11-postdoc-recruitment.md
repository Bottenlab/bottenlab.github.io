The BottenLab is hiring a **Postdoctoral Associate**! We're looking for someone to join our team in FIU's [Center for Children and Families](https://ccf.fiu.edu/) and help us figure out how pubertal hormones shape the developing brain.

> How does pubertal neuroendocrine plasticity confer vulnerability/resilience to neurotoxic effects of environmental exposure?

### What you'd work on

This position will contribute to ongoing studies of pubertal hormones in adolescent brain development, funded by Dr. Bottenhorn's NIH BRAIN Initiative K99/R00. We use deep learning to model pubertal neuroendocrine plasticity in population-level neuroimaging data and explainable AI to interpret those models. You'd work with data from the [Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development Study (ABCD Study)](https://docs.abcdstudy.org/latest/study/) and other existing developmental neuroimaging datasets, as well as new data from a dense, longitudinal pilot study. You'll lead first-author manuscripts and help develop new funding applications to build on these resources.

### Who we're looking for

We welcome applicants from a wide variety of backgrounds, including (but not limited to) epidemiology, biostatistics, neuroscience and neuroimaging, exposure assessment, bio-/neuroinformatics, developmental psychology, precision medicine, and data science. Doctoral training in epidemiology or biostatistics with experience analyzing big data is preferred, but exceptional candidates from other backgrounds are very much encouraged to apply. A PhD is requried prior to starting the position, but ABD candidates are encouraged to apply. Preferred skills include neuroimaging data analysis and machine learning in Python and/or R.

If you're excited about hormones and the developing brain but don't check every box, please apply anyway. Seriously.

### What we offer

- **Funding:** 2 years, with the possibility of extension pending successful grant applications.
- **Career development:** This position is designed to launch the career of a junior investigator, with mentorship in setting career directions and priorities, grant writing (including your own fellowship or career development award applications), effective presentations, manuscript preparation, and building multidisciplinary research teams.
- **Open science, all the way down:** Preregistration, public code, shared data, and reproducible pipelines are standard practice in the BottenLab.
- **A supportive lab culture:** We value ambition, curiosity, kindness, and accountability, with clear expectations and regular check-ins so nobody has to figure everything out alone. Team science is the best science.
- **Miami!** FIU is a Carnegie R1 university in a vibrant, global city, and home to a site of the ABCD Study.

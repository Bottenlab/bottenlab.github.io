We study how hormones and environments shape the brain, using population neuroimaging, deep learning, and dense longitudinal designs. We value curiosity, kindness, accountability, and open science. Team science is the best science, and we want people who share the load and lift each other up.
 
If you're excited about this work but don't check every box, reach out anyway. Seriously.
 
## Postdoctoral Associate
 
We're hiring a postdoc in FIU's [Center for Children and Families](https://ccf.fiu.edu/) to study pubertal hormones and adolescent brain development, supported by Dr. Bottenhorn's NIH BRAIN Initiative K99/R00. You'd use deep learning and explainable AI with data from the [ABCD Study](https://docs.abcdstudy.org/latest/study/), other developmental neuroimaging datasets, and a new dense, longitudinal pilot study. You'd also lead first-author papers and develop your own grant applications.
 
- **Funding:** 2 years, with possible extension
- **Background:** Epidemiology, biostatistics, neuroscience, data science, developmental psychology, and related fields are all welcome. Experience with neuroimaging and machine learning in Python and/or R is a plus.
- **Start date:** ASAP. ABD candidates are encouraged to apply.
**To apply:** Submit to **Job Opening ID 538230** at [careers.fiu.edu](https://careers.fiu.edu) with your CV, a statement of research interests and fit, and contact information for three references. [Full details here.]({{ site.baseurl }}/postdoc-recruitment/)
